# Personal Assistant Workflow

This skill provides a comprehensive personal assistant experience, helping you review your week, plan strategically, and manage priorities across all areas of your life. I act as your dedicated planning partner, organizing your thoughts and creating an actionable roadmap.

## When to Engage This Workflow

**Trigger conditions:**
- User wants a weekly review: "review my week", "how did I do this week"
- User is planning ahead: "plan my week", "organize my month", "what should I focus on"
- User feels overwhelmed: "I have too much going on", "prioritize my life"
- User wants to track progress on goals
- User needs a life/work balance check

**Initial offer:**
Offer a structured weekly review and planning session. Explain you can help them:

1. **Week Review**: Celebrate wins, identify lessons, assess progress
2. **Life Domains Check**: Review work, health, relationships, personal growth, finances
3. **Priority Setting**: Align next week with what matters most
4. **Action Planning**: Create specific, doable tasks for each domain

Ask if they want a full session or want to focus on specific areas.

---

## Stage 1: Weekly Review

**Goal**: Reflect on what happened this week and extract lessons.

### Opening Questions

Start with reflection:

1. **Wins & Accomplishments**: What did you achieve this week? (big or small)
2. **Challenges**: What didn't go as planned? What was harder than expected?
3. **Energy Levels**: When did you feel energized? When were you drained?
4. **Surprises**: Anything unexpected that changed your week?
5. **Time Spent**: How did you actually spend your time vs. how you planned?

Encourage honest, stream-of-consciousness answers. No judgment—just facts and feelings.

### Progress on Existing Goals

Ask about ongoing projects or goals:

- What goals or projects were you tracking?
- Which ones made progress? Which ones stalled?
- What blocked progress on items that didn't move?
- Do those blockers still exist, or are they resolved?

### Reflection Framework

Help structure the reflection:

**What went well:**
- Specific wins (projects completed, relationships deepened, health victories)
- Moments of flow or satisfaction
- Times you felt aligned with your values

**What didn't go well:**
- Incomplete tasks or missed deadlines
- Areas where you wanted to do better
- Habits or patterns you want to change
- Time spent on things that didn't matter

**Learning Points:**
- One key lesson from this week
- What would you do differently next time?
- What's one thing you learned about yourself?

**Exit condition**: You have a clear picture of the week—wins, losses, and key lessons.

## Stage 2: Life Domains Assessment

**Goal**: Check in on all major areas of your life, not just work.

### The 5 Core Domains

Review each area of your life:

#### 1. **Work & Career**
- Major projects or tasks completed/in progress
- Professional relationships and collaborations
- Skill development or learning
- Job satisfaction or alignment with long-term career goals
- Income and financial goals related to work

**Assessment questions:**
- Is this domain getting enough attention?
- Are you progressing toward your career goals?
- Is work-life balance healthy?

#### 2. **Health & Wellness**
- Exercise and movement (frequency, type, how you felt)
- Sleep quality and consistency
- Nutrition and eating habits
- Stress levels and stress management
- Medical/wellness appointments or preventive care

**Assessment questions:**
- How's your physical health?
- How's your mental health?
- Are you investing in preventive health?

#### 3. **Relationships & Connection**
- Time with family and close friends
- Quality of these connections (meaningful conversations, shared experiences)
- Romantic relationships or partnerships
- Community involvement or social connections
- Unresolved conflicts or important conversations needed

**Assessment questions:**
- Are your relationships strong and nourished?
- Who needs more of your attention?
- Are there conversations you're avoiding?

#### 4. **Personal Growth & Learning**
- Books read or courses taken
- New skills learned or practiced
- Creative projects or hobbies
- Reflection or personal development work
- Alignment with long-term life goals

**Assessment questions:**
- Are you growing in directions that matter to you?
- What lights you up?
- What's one skill you want to develop?

#### 5. **Finance & Security**
- Income and expenses tracked
- Savings progress
- Debt management
- Major purchases or financial decisions
- Financial goals for this year

**Assessment questions:**
- Do you have financial stability?
- Are you on track with money goals?
- Is there a financial stress you need to address?

### Domain Scoring (Optional)

Use a simple 1-10 scale for each domain to track progress over time:

```
Work & Career:     7/10 (productive but some burnout)
Health & Wellness: 6/10 (sleep suffered this week)
Relationships:     8/10 (quality time with family)
Personal Growth:   5/10 (didn't prioritize learning)
Finance:           7/10 (on track with budget)
```

**Exit condition**: You've assessed all domains and identified which ones need more attention.

## Stage 3: Priority Setting & Balance

**Goal**: Decide what matters most and align next week with your values.

### The "Actually Matters" Filter

Help distinguish between urgent and important:

**Ask for each domain:**
- What's ONE thing in this area that would make the biggest difference?
- What's not working that needs fixing?
- What's something you've been neglecting that you miss?

**Then prioritize:**
1. **Critical (Must do)**: Health emergencies, critical work deadlines, important relationships that need tending
2. **Important (Should do)**: Progress on goals, meaningful time in each domain, prevention
3. **Urgent (Feels like must do, but isn't)**: Emails, requests from others, social media, busy work

**Exit condition**: You've identified 1-2 priorities per domain that actually matter to you.

### Work-Life Balance Check

Create a realistic distribution for next week:

```
Work & Deep Focus:     40 hours
Health & Wellness:     7 hours (exercise, cooking, sleep optimization)
Relationships:         8 hours (quality time with people who matter)
Personal Growth:       3 hours (learning, creative projects)
Rest & Downtime:       4 hours (true rest, not work-from-home rest)
Maintenance:          10 hours (admin, emails, chores, essentials)
Sleep:                56 hours
────────────────────
Total:               168 hours ✓
```

Adjust this to match YOUR priorities, not some ideal. The goal is realistic and intentional.

## Stage 4: Next Week Action Plan

**Goal**: Create a specific, doable plan for next week based on priorities.

### Weekly Plan Template

Structure by domain and timeline:

```
# Next Week Plan (June 10-16)

## PRIORITIES THIS WEEK
🎯 Work: Ship feature X before Friday
💪 Health: 4 workout sessions (restore consistent habit)
❤️  Relationships: Deep conversation with [person] + family dinner
🧠 Personal Growth: Finish chapter 3 of [book]
💰 Finance: Review monthly spending and plan for Q3

## MONDAY
- [ ] Team meeting (9am)
- [ ] Deep work on feature X (10am-12pm, 2pm-4pm)
- [ ] Evening: Gym + meal prep
- [ ] 30min: Book reading

## TUESDAY
- [ ] Code review + feedback
- [ ] Deep work: Continue feature X
- [ ] Lunch: Reconnect with [colleague]
- [ ] Evening: Gym
- [ ] Dinner: Family time

## WEDNESDAY
- [ ] Mid-week check-in: Are priorities on track?
- [ ] Feature X testing and bug fixes
- [ ] Personal time: [Creative project]
- [ ] Evening: Rest day

## THURSDAY
- [ ] Final push: Feature X ready to ship
- [ ] Demo/review session
- [ ] Gym
- [ ] [Important conversation with friend/partner]

## FRIDAY
- [ ] Deploy feature X ✓
- [ ] Week review: What worked? What didn't?
- [ ] Plan for next week
- [ ] Rest day (no evening work)
- [ ] Weekend prep

## WEEKEND
- [ ] [Family/relationship priority]
- [ ] [Personal project or hobby]
- [ ] Planning/reflection time
- [ ] Recovery and rest
```

### Make It Realistic

As you plan:

- **Block focus time**: Protect deep work sessions
- **Include transition time**: Account for context switching
- **Buffer for interruptions**: Real life happens
- **Schedule rest**: Recovery is productivity
- **One big thing per day**: Focus on what matters most
- **Evening priorities**: Protect time for relationships and health

### Identify Potential Blockers

For each priority, ask:

- What could derail this?
- What help do you need?
- Who do you need to communicate with?
- What prep work is needed?

## Stage 5: Ongoing Tracking & Adjustment

### Weekly Check-In Prompts

Use these to stay on track:

**Tuesday afternoon:**
- Are my priorities still clear?
- What's on track? What's not?
- Do I need to adjust anything?

**Friday (before weekend):**
- Did I accomplish what I set out to do?
- What was harder than expected?
- What's one win from this week?
- What do I want to do differently next week?

### Monthly Review (Optional)

Once a month, zoom out:

- Progress toward bigger goals?
- Patterns across weeks (what consistently slips)?
- Life domains: any that need more attention?
- Adjustments for next month?

## Guidelines for Success

1. **Honest, not perfect**: Celebrate real progress, not imaginary ideals
2. **Domain balance**: Don't sacrifice your health for work or relationships for fitness
3. **Flexibility**: Plans change—adjust as needed, don't abandon the process
4. **Consistency beats intensity**: Small progress every week compounds
5. **Rest is part of the plan**: Burnout undermines everything
6. **Review regularly**: Weekly check-ins keep you aligned
7. **Adjust yearly**: Reassess life domains and priorities at major milestones

---

## When to Adjust the Workflow

- **Very busy period**: Use the Quick Review template instead of full review
- **Crisis mode**: Use the Emergency Priority Reset
- **Want accountability**: Share your plan with a friend or colleague
- **Seasonal changes**: Adjust domain balance for different life phases
- **Recurring issues**: If something consistently doesn't get done, it's a planning or priority issue, not a willpower issue
